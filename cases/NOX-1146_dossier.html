BRIEF FACTS OF THE CASE:

On 12-11-2026 at approximately 07:35 HRS, the control room received a call from Mrs. Lakshmi Kamble stating that her employer, Mrs. Priya Rao (Age 62), was lying unresponsive in her bed at Flat 804, Sea View Towers. The complainant noted that the sliding glass door to the sea-facing balcony was shattered and the master bedroom had been completely ransacked, with clothes and jewelry boxes thrown onto the floor.

Patrol Unit Charlie-4 was dispatched and arrived at 07:42 HRS. They secured the premises and confirmed the victim was deceased. No pulse was found. The body was cold to the touch. Rigor mortis was present. No visible blood or signs of a physical struggle were present on the victim, which appeared inconsistent with a violent home invasion. The Crime Branch was subsequently notified.

SECTION 02: INITIAL CRIME SCENE REPORT

CRIME SCENE INVESTIGATION UNIT

2.1 PHYSICAL LAYOUT

Flat 804 is a 3-BHK sea-facing apartment. The main entrance door was locked from the inside (deadbolt engaged) when the housekeeper arrived. She used her spare key to enter. The living room, kitchen, and guest bedrooms were undisturbed.

The master bedroom, located at the rear of the apartment facing the ocean, was the locus of the incident. A large sliding glass door connects this room to a balcony.

2.2 POINT OF ENTRY

The balcony sliding glass door was shattered near the locking mechanism. The glass shards fell entirely INSIDE the bedroom, confirming the force originated from the exterior (balcony). A 4-foot crowbar or similar prying tool mark was found on the aluminum frame.

2.3 BEDROOM CONDITIONS

The wooden dresser opposite the bed was ransacked. Three drawers were pulled out completely and dumped onto the carpet. A velvet jewelry box was found empty.

Distinct muddy footprints (pattern matching a generic sports sneaker, approx size 9) were identified. The tracks lead directly from the shattered balcony door to the dresser, pivot sharply, and lead back out to the balcony. Crucially, no footprints approach the bed.

2.4 VICTIM OBSERVATIONS

The victim, Priya Rao, was found in the center of a king-sized bed. She was lying on her back, covered to the chest by a duvet. Her eyes were closed. Her facial expression was relaxed. There were no defensive wounds on her hands or arms. No petechial hemorrhaging in the eyes. Lividity (pooling of blood) was fixed on her posterior, indicating her body had not been moved since death.

On the bedside nightstand, investigators found a glass of water (half empty), reading glasses, and two empty 10-tablet blister packs of Zolpidem (a prescription sedative).

5.2 INTERPRETATION

The blood concentration of Zolpidem (210 ng/mL) is significantly above the therapeutic range and falls into the toxic/fatal range, particularly for an elderly patient. This concentration is consistent with the acute ingestion of at least 20mg to 30mg of Zolpidem within a short time frame.

5.3 CAUSE OF DEATH

Death is due to respiratory depression secondary to acute Zolpidem toxicity.

SECTION 09: INTERROGATION TRANSCRIPT

SUSPECT: KIRAN "SPIDER" PATIL

Q: You were caught with Priya Rao's jewelry. We know you broke into Flat 804. We have your footprints. You are facing a murder charge.
A: Look! I'll admit the theft. I'm a cat burglar. That's what I do. But I didn't kill anyone! I swear to God!
Q: Then explain what happened.
A: It was late. Really late. Past 2:30 in the morning. I scaled the drain pipe. I picked the 8th floor because it was dark. I popped the glass door with my pry bar and stepped inside.
Q: And then you attacked the old woman in the bed.
A: No! I didn't even go near the bed! I went straight for the wooden dresser. I pulled the drawers out, grabbed the jewelry boxes, and stuffed the gold in my pockets.
Q: And she just slept through you smashing glass and throwing drawers?
A: That's what I thought at first! I was freezing, waiting for her to wake up and scream. But she didn't move. She didn't even breathe. I looked over at the bed. The street light was shining on her face. She was completely pale. Sir, she was already dead. She was dead before I even got there!
