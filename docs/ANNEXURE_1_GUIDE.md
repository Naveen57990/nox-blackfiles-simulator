# NAGAR-BOT: Annexure 1 & Team Submission Guide
**Competition:** Hack for Social Cause (VBYLD 2027) | **Organizers:** MoYAS & IIT Bombay

---

## 1. Official Document Naming Standard

The MY Bharat portal enforces a strict naming convention for uploaded PDF documents:
$$\mathbf{<State>\_<TeamName>\_<DocumentName>.pdf}$$

### Official Upload File Checklist
1. **7-Slide Presentation Deck:**
   * Example: `Karnataka_TeamCivix_PresentationDeck.pdf` (Max 10 MB, strictly 7 slides, instruction slide removed).
2. **Completed & Signed Annexure 1:**
   * Example: `Karnataka_TeamCivix_Annexure1_Declaration.pdf`
3. **Student ID Proofs:**
   * Example: `Karnataka_TeamCivix_StudentID_Member1.pdf` (Self-attested copy of college ID for each member).
4. **Demonstration Video (Preferred):**
   * Upload on portal or YouTube Unlisted link: `3-5 mins`, `MP4/MOV`, $\ge 720p$, $\le 80\text{ MB}$.

## 2. Annexure 1 Form Fields Breakdown

### Part A: Team Information
* **Team ID:** `[Your registered Team ID on MY Bharat Portal]`
* **Team Name:** `[Must match portal registration exactly]`
* **State / UT:** `[State/UT represented]`
* **Thematic Area:** `Governance & Civic Technology` / `AI, GIS & Emerging Technologies`
* **Problem Statement Title:** `NAGAR-BOT: Low-Cost Civic Issue Observation, Work-Order Automation & Verification System`

### Part B: Team Member Details (Up to 3 Members)
* **Full Name** (as in Aadhaar/Govt ID)
* **Institution Name, Affiliation & AISHE Code** (Verify college AISHE code on https://aishe.gov.in)
* **Course / Degree & Academic Year** (e.g. B.Tech Computer Science, 3rd Year)
* **Student ID Number**
* **Date of Birth & Age** (Age between 18–29 as of reference date)
* **Email ID & Phone Number** (Same as registered on MY Bharat)
* **Government Photo ID Type & Number** (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Driving License)
* **Individual Signature**

### Part C: Self-Certification Clauses
* Confirms originality of work and non-infringement of third-party IP.
* Confirms zero plagiarism and accurate institutional details.
* Consents to educational/promotional use by MoYAS and IIT Bombay.

### Part D: Team Lead Certification
* Team Lead signs on behalf of the team with Place and Date.
